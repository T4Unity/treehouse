Meet Tiffany

Tiffany Paynter, co-founder of Treehouse

“Yoga is medicine.”Tiffany’s belief, and the idea Treehouse is built on.

Tiffany has practiced and taught hatha yoga for 14 years and earned her RYT200 teaching certification at the Sivananda Ashram. Her goal is simple: help every student feel better in mind, body, and heart.

She has taught in schools, prisons, and rehab facilities, and she especially loves welcoming people who don’t think yoga is for them. At Treehouse, that spirit shows up in classes like Begin Here, Midday Zen, and R&B Rest & Breathe.

Her dream for the community is that each of us finds a daily practice that heals and nourishes us, on and off the mat. Two years in, the notes on our board suggest it’s coming true.
